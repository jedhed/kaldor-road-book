1 Harnic league ≈ 4km ≈ 2.5 miles. Travelers cover 3 watches (12 hours) per day without fatigue; the route above assumes that pace unless Forced march is ticked, or the maximum is set to 2 watches (8 hours). Where a road and a river both connect two places, the planner takes whichever is faster for the chosen mode — river travel runs at a fixed current-driven rate regardless of terrain. Carts and wagons can't be carried by riverboats except by special arrangement.
